What it does

Everything a studio tracks, in one place

Built inside a working interior design studio in Dhule, then opened up for other firms.

i.

Leads and enquiries

Log every enquiry, see who has been contacted, and follow up before a lead goes cold.

ii.

Proposals and estimates

Send clean proposals and estimates with your studio's own name and logo.

iii.

Daily site reports

Record site progress with photos so clients and your team see the same picture.

iv.

Stage-wise fees

Set fees by stage, send reminders and receipts, and see what is pending at a glance.

v.

Project costing

For turnkey and with-material jobs, track costs already spent and costs still to come.

vi.

Voice entry

Add leads and tasks by speaking in Hindi, Marathi or English. The app asks for anything missing.
